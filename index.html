AI Systems · Next.js 15 · Model Failover · Live at obasi-ai.vercel.app
Aether AI

A web-based AI chat built for developers, with the interesting part underneath: automatic model failover. Every request walks a chain of five AI providers, so a rate limit, a 5xx or a timeout hands the request to the next model mid-flight instead of failing.

Streams over SSE, runs real tool calls including code execution, and logs every attempt to a live diagnostics dashboard so the failover is observable rather than just claimed.

SQL Analytics · PostgreSQL · Power BI · Business Intelligence
NorthStar Workspace Discovery

A business intelligence assignment for a US office-furniture retailer, where the question was not "how many sales" but where is margin leaking, and which lever pulls next? I modelled six joined tables and answered it in SQL — acquisition cost, return on ad spend, repeat rate, category margin against returns, discount-band profitability, and shipping delay traced straight through to support tickets and CSAT.

The output was an executive memo, not just charts.
